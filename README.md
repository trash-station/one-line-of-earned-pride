One line of earned pride: a father who practised the same four chords for a year after his shift, and tonight his daughter dances barefoot in the kitchen while he plays them straight through. The program writes that line to `/output/entry.txt`:

> For a year he practised the same four chords after his shift, and tonight his daughter danced barefoot in the kitchen while he played them straight through.

## Why this line

It is a quiet, earned moment of pride for someone in a scene, not a summary of one. The scene is concrete and seen happening: a kitchen, a daughter dancing barefoot, a father playing. The pride is earned inside the line by effort the reader can feel — a year of practice on the same four chords, fitted in after a working shift — and it is never asserted, congratulated, or explained; no one in the line says he is proud, the playing itself says it. It lands in one read: the pride arrives with the last words, when the four chords finally hold straight through. And it is fresh: no trophy, no podium, no graduation, just a kitchen and a song that took a year to learn.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
